Snapme Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Snapme is a chat and snap-sharing app. This page explains what data we handle. Contact: VVaultweb@gmail.com

Data we collect

  • Account: email address, password (handled by Firebase Authentication), name, username, an optional profile photo and an optional profile background picture. If you change your username, the old one is retired and nobody can use it again. Your name, username, email and photo are visible to people you chat with.
  • Messages: text, photos, videos and voice messages you send, with sender and time (documents can no longer be sent; ones sent with older versions may still be in old chats).
  • Snaps and the Feed: when you post a snap we save its picture (a compressed photo from the camera or your gallery, or two photos when you use the dual camera) or a short video of up to 3 MB that you pick from your gallery (the video is stored in small pieces together with the snap and removed with it; its first picture is the cover), the caption you may write (up to 150 characters), an optional voice vibe of up to 10 seconds, your name and username, and the time. A snap is shown in the Feed for 30 days and is then removed. If your account is Public, your snap appears in the Public feed that every Snapme account can open, and in your friends’ Feed. If your account is Private (the switch at the top of your Profile page), only your friends see it. You can also share a snap with your Inner Circle only; the list of your Inner Circle is saved privately and only you can read it. You can tag up to 5 friends or Public accounts in a snap; their username is saved in the snap and they can see it. People who can see a snap can react to it, and you can delete your own snaps at any time. If a snap is reported, the Snapme admins can open and remove that snap.
  • Memories: every snap you post, and every snap you are tagged in, is also kept in your own Memories (picture, caption, tags and voice vibe) until you delete it: the app never removes a memory by itself. Memories are shown on your Profile page. Only snaps that you post to your Feed are kept: a picture that you only take with the camera is never saved there. Snaps you are tagged in stay private to you. The snaps you posted yourself can be seen on your profile by your friends, and by everybody if your account is Public; you can switch this off in Settings > Safety & Visibility > Show my Memories on my profile. You can delete a memory at any time.
  • Log in with a username: so that you can type your username instead of your email on the login page, your email address is saved next to your username in a lookup that the login page can read before you are signed in (one username at a time; the list cannot be read). Anybody who knows your exact username can therefore find the email address of your account. You can switch this off in Settings > Safety & Visibility > Log in with my username; the lookup is then deleted. It is also deleted when you change your username or delete your account.
  • Bio: an optional bio of up to 150 characters is saved with your profile and is shown on your profile to people who can open it (everybody if your account is Public, only your friends if it is Private).
  • Comments: a comment on a snap is saved with the snap (the text, your name, your username and the time) and can be seen by the people who can see that snap. You can delete your own comment, and the owner of a snap can delete any comment on it. Comments are removed with the snap.
  • Notifications: when somebody likes or comments on your snap, or accepts your friend request, a short note (that person’s name and username, what happened, the snap and the comment text) is saved in your account. Only you can read it. You can clear it on the Notifications page, and notes older than 30 days are removed. The Notifications page also lists new snaps from your friends and snaps that tag you (taken from your Feed, nothing extra is saved for them) and tells you when your account is signed in on more than one phone (this comes from your Login activity).
  • Day Streak: a counter of the days in a row you posted a snap is saved with your profile.
  • Push token: if notifications are on, a Firebase Cloud Messaging token of your phone or browser is stored with your account so message notifications can reach you while Snapme is closed. It is deleted when you turn notifications off, log out or delete your account.
  • Location (only if you turn it on): on the Location page you can share where you are with your friends. While sharing is on and Snapme is open, your latitude and longitude are saved and can be seen only by the friends you choose (all of them or selected ones; exact, or rounded to about 1 km). It is deleted when you stop sharing, log out or delete your account. The map pictures are loaded from OpenStreetMap, whose servers can see your IP address and the map area you look at (not your name or your friends).
  • Activity: a green dot shows when you are online, plus typing and read status. You can turn the activity indicator off (then you also stop seeing it for others) and turn seen ticks off on your Profile page. While someone writes to you, Snapme can show a short “is typing to you” popup (you can turn Typing alerts off on your Profile page).
  • Friend suggestions: accounts that joined recently can be suggested to other people on the Add friend page (“Find friends”). The suggestion shows your name, your username and, if your profile photo is visible to everyone, your photo. Private accounts are suggested too, but adding a private account only sends a friend request. An account that is waiting to be deleted is not suggested. Each person can hide any suggestion on their own phone.
  • Copy on your phone: so Snapme also opens without internet, the app keeps a copy of your chats, friends and messages that were loaded on your phone, plus the app itself. It stays on your phone until you clear Snapme’s data or uninstall the app.
  • Reports and suspensions: when you report a user or message, we store the report (your name, the other person’s name, a short excerpt of the reported text and the time). It can be read by the Snapme admins, who can open that one reported message; admins cannot browse anybody’s chats. If an account is suspended for breaking the rules, the reason, the date and the end time (if there is one) are stored with the account, together with any appeal you send, any warning the admins send you and private admin notes about the account. The admins keep a log of the actions they take and can give an account a verified badge.
  • Login activity: device name, login and logout times and last-active time, shown only to you in Settings. You can turn the activity indicator off.
  • Blocks: who you block, so blocked people cannot see your username, email, photo or online status.

Device permissions

Camera, microphone and location are used only when you use the related feature (take a snap or photo, record a voice message or voice vibe, pick or save photos, show or share your location on the Location page). Notifications are optional. On Android, Snapme can keep running in the background (a small notification is shown) so message notifications can reach you. When the app screen is closed it opens Snapme in a hidden page inside the app to listen for new messages; that page uses the same account and stores nothing extra. You can turn this off on your Profile page > Notifications.

How data is used

Only to run the chat service: deliver messages, show your profile to people you chat with, and keep the service safe. We do not sell data and do not show ads.

Where data is stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Google processes it as our service provider.

Retention

Every message has an expiry time (you can choose After seen, Today (24 hours) or 7 days per chat). Expired messages are hidden and removed from the database. Snaps are shown for 30 days and are removed after that; notifications are kept for 30 days. Memories stay until you delete them. Profile data stays until you delete your account. Comments you wrote on other people’s snaps stay until that snap is removed.

Your choices and account deletion

You can delete your account in the app: Profile page > Account > Delete account. The account is deleted 7 days after you ask; the app shows the exact date. If you log in again before that date, the deletion is cancelled. While it is pending your account is hidden from search and friend suggestions. After the 7 days, your login, profile (name, username, photos, about), username, friend requests, blocks, shared location, snaps (with their comments), notifications, Memories and one-to-one chats are removed from our database, and you are removed from groups. Messages you sent in groups may remain until they expire. You can also request deletion by email; see delete-account.html.

Children

Snapme is not for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change.
