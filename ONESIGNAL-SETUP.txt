Snapme - notifications with OneSignal + a free Cloudflare Worker  (Round 17)
=============================================================================
Result: when Snapme is CLOSED (swiped away from the recent apps list) these still arrive as phone notifications, with the Snapme sound:
   new message  .  new snap of a friend  .  friend request  .  like  .  comment  .  your request was accepted  .  admin-panel push (as before)
Cost: 0. No card. Expo stays as a second route (messages and snaps), nothing you did for Expo is lost.

HOW IT WORKS (so you know what you are setting up)
  - Your phone registers with OneSignal after login (OneSignal calls it by your Firebase user id).
  - When somebody sends you a message / posts a snap / likes / comments / sends a request, HIS phone tells the small free "Worker" (a tiny
    program on Cloudflare). The Worker checks that it really happened (it reads Firestore), checks your settings (muted chat, notifications
    off, blocked) and asks OneSignal to deliver the notification to YOUR phone. OneSignal / Google deliver it even if Snapme is closed.
  - The secret keys live ONLY inside the Worker and inside the OneSignal / Cloudflare websites. Never in the app, never in GitHub files,
    never in a chat (not even with me).

(The OneSignal and Cloudflare websites rename buttons now and then. If a button has another name, take the closest one.)

WHAT YOU NEED: a phone browser (Chrome; if a site looks broken, menu > "Desktop site"), your Google/Firebase login, a Cloudflare account (free).

======================================  GOOD TO KNOW  ================================================================
  - Switch it off for everybody at any time: admin panel > Controls > Features > "OneSignal + Worker push". Phones then use Expo again
    (messages and snaps only).
  - If the Worker cannot be reached, messages and snaps go through Expo automatically. Likes, comments and requests have no second route.
  - A phone that has not registered with OneSignal yet (old APK, never logged in after the update) is served by Expo, so nobody gets two
    notifications and nobody is left out.
  - The notification is triggered by the phone of the person who DID the thing (like, message ...). If his phone loses the internet in the
    very second after sending, that one notification is not sent (the message itself is saved and shows when you open Snapme).
  - Free limits: Cloudflare Workers 100,000 requests a day; OneSignal free plan is enough for push notifications.
  - Text in the notification: set the Worker variable SHOW_TEXT = false (type "Text") to show only "New message" instead of the message text.
  - Limits that NO app can remove: "Force stop" in Android settings blocks every push until the app is opened once; Samsung / Xiaomi / Oppo /
    Vivo "sleeping apps" can block pushes of apps that were not opened for days (Phone Settings > Apps > Snapme > Battery > Unrestricted, and
    remove Snapme from "Sleeping apps").
  - Not tested by me on a real phone: the OneSignal plugin inside the APK and the Cloudflare deployment. Tested here: the Worker program (79 checks
    with a fake OneSignal / Firestore), the app side (31 checks), the build scripts and the Java code against stubs. If something does not arrive,
    tell me which step of the TEST part failed and what the Worker address shows in the browser (step B6).
