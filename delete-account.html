Delete your Snapme account

In the app

Open Snapme > Profile > Account > Delete account, then enter your password. Your account is deleted 7 days later (the app shows the exact date). If you log in again before then, the deletion is cancelled and nothing is lost.

By email

If you cannot open the app, email VVaultweb@gmail.com from your registered address with the subject "Delete my Snapme account" and include your username.

What is deleted

After the 7 days your login and profile (name, username, photos, about), friend requests, blocks, shared location, snaps, Memories and one-to-one chats are deleted, and you are removed from groups. Your username becomes available again. While the deletion is pending, nobody can find you by username. Messages you sent in groups may remain until they expire (default 24 hours).
